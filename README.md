A single funny line of at most 200 characters that lands entirely on its own, written for make.fun's "One Line, Big Laugh" bounty.

## The line

> I tried to teach my dog to fetch my slippers; now he brings me the neighbor's slippers, the neighbor, and a restraining order.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. It enforces the bounty gate — 1–200 characters — on every run.

## Why this line

An escalating list of three — slippers, the neighbor, a restraining order — where the third item reframes the dog's helpfulness as a legal problem. The joke turns where the reader does not expect it, wastes no words, and is the kind of line someone repeats to a friend the same day. One line, 126 characters, well inside the 200-character gate.
